# Zaun 990 playtest checklist

## Launch and shared setup

Use the development playset with Runeterra, Quarior and this local Azir checkout. Start in English. The preview resolved to a fresh ZAU start with `-start_tag=ZAU`. To run the test with debug logging, close the previous game session and use:

```powershell
pwsh -File tools/launch-hoi4.ps1 -Mode Fresh -Country ZAU -GameDebug
```

1. Confirm Vander leads Zaun on the 990 bookmark. Open **Politics → Balance of Power**. Check the original panorama and both sides of the native bar.
2. Complete **Meet at the Last Drop → Inspect the Air Shafts → Reopen the Entresol Freight Lift → Seat the Ward Engineers**. In Piltover, complete **The City of Progress → Commission the Canal Survey → Appoint the Crossing Delegation**. Use a second player for both-country steps, or switch countries in a disposable single-player debug save while paused.
3. As Piltover, select **Decisions → The Twin Cities → Commission the Canal Survey** for 20 PP and send the inspection order. As Zaun, answer the event. Save before either side declares its crossing policy.
4. From that checkpoint, run the four declaration pairs in [Piltover and Zaun crossing crisis](PIL%20and%20ZAU%20Crossing%20Crisis.md). Verify the declaration price of 25 PP each, exploit price of 30 PP, response price of 20 PP, detour price of 65 PP and reopen price of 15 PP. Check that the original four outcomes still settle as described there.

## Ward Compact

1. As Zaun, complete **Standardize the Foundries → Charter the Ward Compact → Apprentices of the Lanes**. Under **Decisions → Zaun's Civic Agenda**, fund the **Sump Clinic** and **Promenade Workshop** for 25 PP and 30 days each. Check that occupying state 49 or 50 during its matching project cancels that project.
2. For the cooperative run, choose **Share the Hoist Register and Reserve Freight Space** as Zaun and **Publish the Survey and Open the Freight Register** as Piltover. Complete **Fund the Lower Approach** and **Fund the Upper Approach**, then use **Reopen the Crossing**. Confirm **Joint Freight** is operating and both cities retain their approach states.
3. Start **Begin a Ward Service Cycle** for 20 PP with no delivered regional agreements. It should show a 90-day mission. Save midway and reload. Finish with 49 and 50 continuously owned and controlled by Zaun and the route running. Expect the service completion event, **Ward Services in Operation** for 180 days and access to **Charter the Ward Services**. Reload and interrupt one ward or the freight route mid-cycle. Expect cancellation and no completion reward.
4. In a separate run or after the first service spirit expires, fulfill both regional contracts and start the cycle again. Expect 60 days with either or both agreements live. Repeat after withdrawal of the last agreement, before starting a new cycle. Expect 90 days from the domestic supply route. Test a 90-day cycle on the active domestic detour as well.
5. Complete **Charter the Ward Services**. At the deep cooperative range, enact **Local Purchasing** for 25 PP in the Balance of Power panel. Check the political advisor slot for the **Ward Engineer Delegation**, and check the policy's workshop efficiency and heavy procurement penalty. From the pre-charter checkpoint, confirm the freight declaration alone cannot unlock the policy.
6. Interrupt the Accord by removing Piltover's control of state 52 while Zaun holds 49 and 50. Advance a day. **Dispatch Ward Recovery Crews** should appear under **Zaun's Civic Agenda**. Complete its 30-day dispatch for 60 days of repair crews. Repeat from the checkpoint and pay 50 PP for **Renegotiate the Ward Purchasing Charter**. At day 60 the policy ends, a hired engineer delegation leaves office, and the ward recovery decision closes.

## Glasc Directorate

1. Reload the opening and complete **Standardize the Foundries → Accept Glasc Credit**. Accept Renata's investment. Complete **The Foundry Receivership** and approve the consolidation in **Hostile Takeover**. Finish **Renata's Directorate** and confirm Renata succeeds Vander through the existing promotion.
2. Set both cities to their exclusive crossing commitments to produce mutual hardening. As Zaun, complete **Route Freight to Lower Landing**, then run **Run the Lower Landing Detour**. Confirm Zaun owns and controls 55. In **Zaun's Civic Agenda**, complete **Build the Lower Landing Freight Works** for 35 PP over 60 days. It adds one infrastructure level where capacity permits.
3. After **Seat the Ward Engineers**, send either regional offer and accept it as the matching counterpart. Keep the route open, 55 in Zaun's hands and the worksite under its signatory for the full 90-day mission. Acceptance alone should leave credit and procurement locked. Try losing control of 55 or the foreign site mid-delivery, then restore it. The interrupted shipment should cancel and need a new agreement.
4. With a fulfilled, live contract, finish **Underwrite the Directorate**. At the deep coercive range, enact **Exclusive Purchasing** in the Balance of Power panel for 25 PP. Check contract-backed industrial credit, the consumer-goods cost and the **Glasc Purchasing Office** in the political advisor slot. In **Zaun's Civic Agenda**, place a 35 PP equipment order and run it for 45 days. Check the logistics ledger for 60 additional support equipment, accounting for concurrent reinforcement.
5. Start a second equipment order and pay 50 PP for **Wind Down Exclusive Purchasing**. Pending offers and the equipment order close at the start, undelivered shipments cancel on their next update and credit stops. At day 60 the purchasing policy ends and its advisor is unavailable. A completed service agreement can still appear on the counterpart's side. Reload and run the same equipment-order test by withdrawing the final live contract. Expect cancellation without a stockpile grant.

## Firelight Emergency Federation

1. Complete **Charter the Ward Compact**. From the crossing checkpoint, have Zaun share the hoist register and Piltover put the crossing under Council permit. As Piltover, choose **Impose Exclusive Freight Permits**. Confirm Zaun receives the victim event. As Zaun, select **Close the Hoist Register and Divert Freight**. Check that the branch also opens in a separate mutual-hardening run. In the inverse betrayal, where Zaun exploits Piltover, check that **Hear the Firelight Delegation** stays closed.
2. Complete **Hear the Firelight Delegation** and **Route Freight to Lower Landing**. **Invoke the Firelight Emergency Mandate** for 25 PP under **Zaun's Civic Agenda**. Save during the 60-day term and reload. Keep 49 and 50 under Zaun's ownership and control. Select **Run the Lower Landing Detour** and hold 55 before the term ends. Choose **Ratify the Firelight Emergency Federation** in the vote. Expect Vander to remain leader, the temporary mandate spirit to end and **Dispatch Federation Repair Crews** to open. New Zaunite regional offers close under this route. Previously accepted agreements continue subject to their gates.
3. Reload and take the return-to-wards choice. Repeat by running out the term without a working detour, then by losing 49 or 50 during the term. In all three cases the mandate should end, ward authority should resume and federation repairs should remain unavailable. Test the 30-day repair decision after ratification with and without an interruption of the detour.

## Regional offers and multiplayer isolation

From a save after both cities' engineering or delegation focuses, use the **Zaunite Service Agreements** and Piltover **Regional Agreements** decision categories. Record the offer day, counterpart owner and controller, balance panels and stockpiles. For control-only cases, change the controller through occupation in a disposable debug save and confirm the state panel still shows the original owner. For ownership cases, use `setowner PLT 28`, `setowner SHU 118` or `setowner PLT 55` in a disposable single-player save and reload afterward.

| Case | Steps and expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Independent same-country offers | Send both Zaun offers together. Accept **Offer Kumungu Filtration** as IXT and refuse **Offer Crystal Scar Reclamation Services** as CRY. Only the Kumungu delivery should open. Repeat with the outcomes reversed. |
| Independent offers from both cities | Send Zaun's Kumungu offer and Piltover's **Request the Kumungu Survey** before answering either. As IXT, accept one and refuse the other. Repeat in reverse order. Do the same as CRY with Zaun reclamation and Piltover's **Propose a Crystal Scar Registry**. Each response should affect only its sender. |
| Late reply and resend | Save and reload while one offer is open. Let the game advance 30 days without accepting it. Try to accept at expiry, then wait for the popup to close and the 35-day resend cooldown to lift. Only a fresh offer can create a delivery. Repeat with an immediate refusal and verify its reply creates no mission or territorial change. |
| Start before freight opens | Offer and accept while at peace with both sites secure, before any route is operating. No delivery timer runs yet. Reopen the crossing or activate the detour. The accepted order should enter its 90-day delivery then. Allow AI Zaun to run the same opening and check that it waits for a viable delivery route before sending offers. |
| Control and peace validation | Try sending with 55 or the counterpart worksite out of its signatory's control. Restore control, send, then change control before the answer. The answer must not start delivery while invalid. Repeat during delivery and after a completed contract, and repeat with a war between Zaun and the counterpart. A completed agreement's benefits suspend during invalid conditions. |
| Counterpart withdrawal | Accept both Zaun offers. In IXT choose **Withdraw from the Zaun Filtration Agreement** while the mission is running. Confirm only the Ixtali mission is canceled. After completion, withdraw the CRY agreement through its own decision. Future benefits relying on it should end. Separately revoke one of two fulfilled agreements while leaving the other valid and check that the remaining contract can still back credit and filters. Piltover and Shurima agreements should be unaffected. |
| AI response | In separate runs let IXT or CRY answer under AI control. IXT should favor supervised delivery on the Ward Compact route. The Scar's acceptance reserves custody. If Zaun takes exclusive loading slots while a pre-route offer is pending, compare a set of repeated responses with and without active priority freight to check that refusal is weighted more strongly during exploitation. |
| Save and reload | Save separately with an offer pending, an accepted contract awaiting freight, a 90-day shipment running, a completed contract and an active wind-down. After each reload check the timers, spirits, advisor availability and counterpart withdrawal. Only one reply and one delivery reward should take effect for each agreement. |
