## Labor laws · The decree came too late to be enforced

**Belief and Legitimacy, author direction, 26 November 2026.** Add Legitimacy alongside Shurima’s existing emphasis on Belief. For an ordinary Shuriman, Azir’s return raises two questions: who is this emperor, and why has he come back? The Slave Question puts his claim to rule to a practical test. Enslaved families may tolerate delay if freedom is promised and their conditions improve in the meantime. Slavers have the opposite interest. If Azir moves against slavery early, slaver groups may break away and fight him while he is already fighting Xerath. This is a risk, not a predetermined revolt or an alliance with Xerath. The existing Renewed Legitimacy spirit belongs to the assassination aftermath. The playable form of the broader Legitimacy design remains open.

**Question of Slavery** opens the decision category. Shurima starts with `Lawless_slavery` in force. Azir pronounced emancipation before the Ascension disaster. The restored state has to build the courts, employment and enforcement that the decree never had. [Azir’s biography](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/story/champion/azir/) · [Unbound](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/story/xerath-color-story/)

| Law | Cost and debate | Gameplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Child Enslavement Bill | 50 PP, 45 days | Outlaws new claims on children. Greater stability, slower construction. |
| Slave Rights Act | 75 PP, 60 days | Recognizes petitions, family protections and the right to challenge claims. Greater stability, more civilian factories tied up funding oversight. Replaces any slaver licences. |
| Slaver Rights Act | 65 PP, 60 days | Recognizes licensed houses in return for construction labor. Faster building, lower stability. This is an optional detour. The Rights Act can still replace it. |
| Sun Service | 85 PP, 60 days | Pays workers for public works. Faster infrastructure construction for a civilian funding cost. Requires the Rights Act. |
| Enforce the Emancipation Decree | 105 PP, 90 days | Removes the old bondage spirit and folds in the earlier laws. Grants stability and a larger voluntary recruitment pool, with a further 120-day construction transition. |

Shortest abolition route is **255 in-game days** and **315 PP**, plus the time needed to earn PP and complete the existing focus. Only one bill can be debated at a time. Values, the bill order, and the Sun Service’s paid-work interpretation are provisional balance and design choices.

**Author decision, 24 November 2026.** Communities should remember whether Shurima licensed slavers before passing the Slave Rights Act. That history should matter during abolition and after emancipation. The form of the response and its balance are open for author review. Implementation and gameplay verification are pending.

**Azir’s speech, 26 November 2026.** Azir wants to earn the trust of ordinary Shurimans who have good reason to fear what restoring the empire would mean for them, especially communities still facing active slavers in 990. If a family brings evidence that an imperial official protected a slaver, Azir promises to protect the family from retaliation and bring the official to trial. If the charge is proven, the official loses their post, and the family sees that Azir kept his word. The wider community response remains open.

**Sivir’s speech, 26 November 2026.** Sivir wants the family to be able to bring its case before a council its own community trusts. That council should have the power to protect the family from retaliation and put the imperial official on trial even if the emperor refuses to hear the case. The town guard answers to the council and can remove a convicted official even if the emperor orders the official to stay.

**The Slave Question as Azir, 26 November 2026.** Slavers are active in 990. Azir can accept abolition, but his preferred course is to retain slavery until he judges the restored empire capable of functioning without it. His privilege shapes whose readiness he considers. On another player route he can conclude that emancipation was a mistake and claim that enslavement benefits even the people he deems incapable of using their freedom. These are Azir’s political judgments. What would convince him the empire is ready remains open.

**The Slave Question as Sivir, 26 November 2026.** Sivir is an abolitionist at her core. Her concerns extend to trafficking in humanoids, forced and penal labor, prisons and the exploitation of animals. A player can pursue a policy under which freedom can be bought. A distinct, rarer and more extreme position makes enslaved people earn their freedom. On her more exclusionary alternatives, Sivir can narrow whom she recognizes as an equal and leave exploitation outside her reforms. The author’s examples include Nami, Ionian vastaya and beings native to Targon. Those distinctions express Sivir’s choices on those routes. The laws, eligibility rules and consequences of each route remain to be worked out.

## Proposal · The Great Hunt

**Entry.** Finish **Big Game** as Nazumah to open K’Sante’s expedition. He follows reports out of Nazumah and into the wider hunt. Ixtal follows Rengar and the Void follows Kha’Zix through their own invitations. Keep the older Azir–Nasus–Renekton preparation events separate. A shared hunt record should hold the current round and result, with each participant receiving only their own scene.

**Three rounds, separate perspectives.** Each playable round offers two tactics with a clue in the prose. Give one point for reading that clue well. Players can follow their hunter closely or choose an option like **Trust Rengar’s instincts** and let that hunter act independently. Give unanswered prompts a deadline and resolve them through the independent rule so play never waits indefinitely for a disconnected or unresponsive player.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The trail | Read Tope’s notes and protect travelers, or race after the first tracks. | Study the torn tracks, or charge toward a roar. | Observe from cover, or commit to a tempting first strike. | The same disturbed caravan route from ground level, a rock ledge, and a POV under the scrub. |
| The crossing | Choose between a defensible passage and following tracks into unstable ground. | Read the wind for a flanking route, or follow visible marks. | Test how the others react to movement, or commit to a lure. | The same dry wash and treeline, with a different sign visible in each panel. |
| The encounter | Use the terrain to shield others and isolate the threat, or take a direct contest. | Withdraw to draw the quarry into reach, or attack while it expects him. | Adapt to an opening left by the two hunters, or rely on the trick they have already seen. | One confrontation in the borderlands, three camera angles and three finishing illustrations. |

**Resolution.** Make one synchronized weighted draw after the final round. Guided hunters with 0, 1, 2, or 3 correct choices get weights of 1, 2, 4, or 8. An independent hunter always gets weight 4, with its final winning chance capped at 50%. Normalize the weights, then redistribute any excess above the cap to the other entrants. Against one guided hunter with three correct answers, the independent hunter has a one-in-three chance. Against a guided hunter who missed every clue, the independent hunter still has at most a 50/50 chance. With three hunters going it alone, each has one-third. Require at least two entrants for the competitive ending, with local reconnaissance and its own reward when only one is available. These are proposal values.

**Stakes.** Show the risk before entry. One hunter wins and takes the unique trophy, prestige, or adaptation. Under the lethal version you described, the other competing hunters die. Every participating country can still earn something from the expedition, such as field research, survivor testimony, or equipment recovered on the way. Before enabling deaths, check every character-specific focus and decision for a fallback so the hunt resolves its stakes without stranding the rest of a campaign. Ixtal’s playable Rengar and the full event set are still prerequisites. ## Xerath and Renekton

- **Renekton route.** The player can take power and become more consumed by the madness. Show that in what he can do, not only in narration. His civil-war faction should get the warbands and initial stores he can credibly command, with those resources coming out of Xerath’s side where feasible. Preserve the middle route as an unstable accommodation, with Xerath still using Renekton and Renekton still able to hold the warbands.
- **Concurrent wars.** The opening Shurima war can still be running when the civil war starts. First test who inherits participants, occupation and peace conference eligibility when the split occurs. A targeted settlement rule would need to preserve Shurima’s actual wartime gains while preventing a conference from deciding the still-live XER–Renekton succession by accident. The proposed 90-day truce is rejected. The existing SHU offer remains disabled.
- **Assassination.** Proposal for review: let Xerath declare a primary target and pay for preparation. The choice can signal or slightly weight the default roll, with no authority to remove the other player’s character. Shurima gets a warning and a timed response. It can accept the risk or pay protection costs to select a costly outcome that keeps Azir and Sivir available. Give a human-controlled SHU the same protection against both a human XER and an AI XER. Preserve the core unification branches in all cases. The current outcome script has not been changed.

After the Xer'Sai leaves, Shurima has 40 quiet days. Afterward another paid investigation can uncover another attack. The end of the cooldown does not spawn one. **Implemented in the development checkout.** The first rift sends a one-time country event to SHU. Startup backfills reports for saves past Rift Rapture, including saves past Open Void Gate, if SHU exists and the report is missing. Saves that already received reports keep their existing progress. The discovery marks a controlled state and starts its matching 180-day mission. Each available response cuts that timer by 30 days. Departure clears the marked state even if control changed, and the 40-day cooldown ends without spawning another attack. Native event timing, reload and multiplayer need player verification.
