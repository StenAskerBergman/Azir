# Shurima in 990

Shurima opens at war with Xerath. Its fifteen deployed formations can fight before an institution gives the country its first research slot. The first practical technology question is how Shurima replaces their losses while recovering the expertise to equip a different kind of army. This dossier follows the existing campaign scripts. Suggested research applications below are design options.

## From inheritance to a field company

1. **Choose an institution or fight first.** Shuriman University and Officer School can each give one research slot without a preceding focus. School of Nasus also requires Nasus serving as an adviser. These three focuses are mutually exclusive. Starting infantry, recon and engineer knowledge is therefore available before a new research project can begin. [Research branch](../../common/national_focus/shurima_focus.txt)
2. **Reconstitute the legions.** Rebirth of the Legions requires control of a state adjacent to state 121 or the fractured-Shurima idea. It unlocks editing of Shuriman Infantry, adds 15 Army XP and 25,000 manpower. The defensive path runs through Defensive Lines and Arm the Defenders. The offensive path runs through Plan Frontlines and Storm Nerimazeth. Either predecessor opens Shuriman Legions, which grants war economy and can bypass if Shurima already has it. [Army branch](../../common/national_focus/shurima_focus.txt)
3. **Muster riders.** Lion Riders follows Shuriman Legions. Finishing it exposes a one-time 25 PP decision for original SHU while training is unknown. The decision grants the hidden training directly. Researching it does not consume one of the newly opened slots. Its reward enables the company. Staffing a template and issuing equipment come afterwards. [Decision category](../../common/decisions/categories/SHU_decision_categories.txt) · [Muster](../../common/decisions/SHU_dec.txt) · [Training](../../common/technologies/azir_runeterra_theme_replacements.txt)
4. **Equip a host division.** One company requires 300 manpower, 40 infantry equipment and 10 support equipment. The opening tech grants already include the relevant equipment families. Choose an editable formation, assign the company to a support slot, and fund reinforcement. Its existing role is reconnaissance with +15% desert movement, zero combat width and no change to base formation speed. A mounted line formation would be a separate design. The muster AI weight is zero because the AI lacks a template using the company. [Lion Rider company](../../common/units/azir_shu_lion_riders.txt) · [Support equipment unlock](../../common/technologies/support.txt)
5. **See the cost in play.** Xerath’s Arcane Followers and Shurima’s Sand Soldiers open with the same ordinary infantry chassis. Ordinary arms can engage either. A research rewrite that gave both supernatural names a high armor threshold would change the balance of the opening war overnight. The neighboring Void creates a second test. Rift Rapture sends reports to SHU. Investigation can lead to one of three timed threats. During an active threat Shurima can spend 500 infantry equipment and 25 PP to prepare defenses, or spend 10,000 manpower under martial law when a division occupies the affected state. Taking 500 from the defensive focus grant would leave 1,000 of its 1,500 pieces for other needs. Lion Riders have no scripted bonus to the encounter timer. [Xerath OOB](../../history/units/XER.txt) · [Rift Rapture](../../common/national_focus/void_focus.txt) · [Reports and investigation](../../events/Shurima_void_events.txt) · [Response costs](../../common/decisions/SHU_dec.txt)

## Combat and the first-pass decision

Use approximately 4 km/h as the initial foot reference. For a proposed armor rating, raise hardness only when the formation deserves a higher share of incoming hard attack. At 30% hardness, HOI4 draws 70% from the opponent’s soft attack and 30% from its hard attack before other combat modifiers. Armor and piercing are a separate comparison. Keep their early values low enough for ordinary opening equipment to fight both sides; validate the result with the actual division totals. A scout’s mount can improve desert movement without giving every soldier in the column a mount.

**Author decision, 26 November 2026.** Replace existing technology lines with the agreed Runeterra ideas first. Shurima keeps its existing equipment families, stockpiles and reinforcement path in this pass. Arm the Defenders still grants 1,500 infantry equipment, and Sand Soldiers still draw from that stockpile. This resolves the implementation choice for the first pass. Consider a separate replenishment system, a different air role or a new rider supply chain in a later pass only where the change would matter in play.

For eventual validation, inspect the SHU opening against XER with the intended playset, check its factories and unassigned orders, then compare the same editable division before and after adding riders on ordinary and desert terrain. Verify the muster survives reload and produces the stated equipment demand. A separate Rift Rapture run should show that spending 500 equipment changes the active investigation, and a two-player run should confirm shared war, stockpile and mission outcomes.
