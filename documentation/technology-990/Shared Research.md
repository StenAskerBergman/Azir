# Shared research in Runeterra, 990 AN

This is a design catalog. “Shared” means two countries may use the same underlying capability and generic sprite. Each country’s starting equipment, training institutions, supply, workshop knowledge, routes and prior decisions determine access. A generic graphic does not grant a technology, make the unit buildable, or put a formation on the map.

[Current technology nodes.csv](Current%20technology%20nodes.csv) lists 707 distinct technology IDs in 18 effective technology files in the modeled local stack. It gives the source layer, file, line, tree folder, dates, outgoing and incoming paths, separately declared dependencies, exclusions, source gates, equipment, units, modules and effects. The owner order is installed HOI4 → Runeterra → Quarior → local Azir, resolving identical relative filenames to the last layer. The CSV includes 149 legacy doctrine records and both designer and fixed-design layouts. Its row count is a count of definitions, not the number of buttons one player sees. Source year and costs describe the input scripts. In particular the existing dates do not establish when a named Runeterran invention occurred. The prerequisite and access discussion in the later redesign appendix concerns a possible second pass. Actual source prerequisites are in the CSV, including effect-only and hidden records. Category bonuses, research paths and caller IDs must be migrated together.

## First pass — replace existing technology lines in place

Use the existing technology ID, path, research cost, year, availability, consumers, effects, stockpiles and templates. The CSV gives proposed English display names for 381 existing IDs. Seven of those are included in the first [English infantry replacement](../../localisation/english/replace/zz_azir_infantry_arms_l_english.yml). The other 374 remain proposals. Every remaining record explicitly retains its current display pending evidence or stays with its existing doctrine/project/route owner. A visible bow on a bonus node can represent a volley drill. It does not let the player produce bows, raise a bow battalion, or reject old equipment. Keep equipment descriptions and UI tooltips honest about the real effect. National visual variations belong to country implementation after this generic pass.

## Training, mastery and prototypes

The installed game separates an XP-purchased **grand doctrine**, a **subdoctrine** slotted into a track, and rewards earned by **mastery** within that track. A national tactical tradition can alter how existing troops fight here without making a weapon appear in storage. The local `mobile_infantry` subdoctrine in [infantry_subdoctrines.txt](../../common/doctrines/subdoctrines/land/infantry_subdoctrines.txt) already uses 100 army XP, a motorized research bonus and mastery rewards including `cavalry_charge`. A redesign must carry that existing bonus to an equivalent applicable research category and preserve any intentional movement/tactic effects. Land, naval, air and special-forces doctrine tracks remain separate from factory designs. Runeterra’s older air/land/naval doctrine files still contain 149 technology records. Check the active UI and country history before migrating any of their calls to modern mastery or promising that those older nodes display.

The industrial proof sequence is **research → eligible equipment → production → stockpile → equipped unit or wing → intended mission**. For facilities it is **access → staffed facility and breakthrough → project → prototype/output → production when permitted**. The existing [Twin Cities projects](../../common/special_projects/projects/azir_twin_cities.txt) and [Xerath battery](../../common/special_projects/projects/azir_xer_siege_battery.txt) illustrate different outputs. The former require production/stockpiles to equip their new companies. The latter project declares a subunit unlock. Each preserves its own AI availability, tag and route conditions. Hidden decision/focus grants such as Shurima’s muster are not research slots.

## Compatibility and residual decisions

- Fixed design versus designer is a presentation and equipment-path distinction across armor (No Step Back), aircraft (By Blood Alone) and ships (Man the Guns). Check both source folders, current availability triggers, module compatibility, designer default templates, equipment roles and associated AI production. Match the playable capability on both layouts where supported.
- The CSV includes country-specific/DLC, hidden, script-granted, prototype, obsolete-year, extreme-technology and legacy doctrine rows. Blank folder means no folder was declared in that record. Incoming paths capture source edges, not the full logic of dependencies, branch gates, country history or who is actually eligible. Country-specific access is a separate review. An unassigned residual remains outside the proposed shared tree.
- The base game’s old category names still feed research bonuses and focus rewards. Inventory starting technologies, equipment variants, unit templates, OOBs, decisions, special projects, unit categories and available equipment before replacing an ID. Register generic technology, equipment, unit and designer sprites separately where their consumers differ. Reuse one generic art family across countries until a national variant is accepted.
- In land testing, compare foot, cavalry, mixed and support-attached formations; separately inspect coverage, piercing, infantry pace and supply. For sea and air, exercise a real invasion, escort, interception, reconnaissance and transport mission on applicable tags. Static inventory does not establish that scripted consumers, visuals, AI and multiplayer behave correctly.

## Later redesign options — outside the in-place pass

Every table in this appendix explores a potential structural change. New stockpiles, unit definitions, speed/hardness changes, different prerequisites, new branches, route access, restriction changes and rewritten effects require a later author decision and implementation plan. None is a prerequisite for first-pass generic display replacement. Source IDs in these tables are analogues for evaluating mechanics.

### Armory and formations

A new formation requires its own unit definition and equipment requirements. An equipment family upgrade only lets factories produce the new pattern. Attachment of a small recon or support element should preserve the host formation’s marching speed. Most line troops begin at ordinary infantry pace. Use armor coverage (hardness) to weight incoming soft and hard attacks; use actual armor and piercing sparingly for a separate penetration interaction. Mounted troops and extraordinary locomotion need explicit justification.

| --- | --- | --- |
