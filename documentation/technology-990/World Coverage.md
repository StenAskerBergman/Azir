# World technology coverage in 990 AN

The January 990 bookmark and state files support **36 countries with at least one uncontested owned state**. Eight more have an ownership candidate on the map. Shakkal is both contested and explicitly offered by the bookmark. A core, a national history file, an OOB, and a selectable country answer different questions. Keep those distinctions when allocating generic technology and commissioning national research art.

## What the register covers

| Opening status | Countries | How to use it |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Owner | 36 | At least one state agrees in the top-level overlay and every highest-layer alternative for that state. A native save would verify control. |
| Possible owner | 8 | All its candidate land has competing ownership. Keep provisional coverage and do not silently assign the disputed state. |
| Core only | 7 | The tag has at least one candidate or top-level core and no owned state. Its activation needs a route or other explicit grant. |
| Registered reserve | 3 | Regional registration and history exist. No opening ownership or core was found. |
| History only | 8 | Regional history exists without a matching regional registration or territory in the inspected stack. Keep these out of the active research-art count. |
| Utility | 2 | The project explicitly excludes Test and Unclaimed Territories from technology provision. |

The register covers 64 candidates. The older [national candidate list](../Runeterra%20Theme%20Replacements.md) has 54 proposed or implemented examples. Its 37 entries in the starting table include bookmarked Shakkal; the current ownership classification places Shakkal with the eight disputed-only owners. Its 44 possible starting tags agree with the union of the owner rows here. The eight unregistered histories and the two utility records explain the difference between 54 and 64.

## How to read the matrix

**E** means a particular practice, asset or institution is established by the evidence in the corresponding cell. **R** means there is specific evidence of access limited to a route, site, practitioner, country activation, or approved research milestone. **A** means absent with affirmative evidence. **?** means unreviewed or insufficient country-specific evidence. A cell marks the narrowest verified example in a capability family. E for transport can mean a stock of convoys. It cannot by itself establish cavalry, roads, shipyards or replacement production.

The [capability ledger](World%20Capability%20Evidence.csv) gives all 512 judgments and their source or boundary. The [country ledger](World%20Country%20Evidence.csv) gives the selected country history, competing files, grants, slots, convoys, OOB file, templates, initial division placements, and state numbers. The [state ledger](World%20State%20Evidence.csv) gives the competing source files for each state, owner and cores.

Column headings correspond, in order, to Armory and formations, Field engineering and siege, Supply and transport, Industry and civil works, Maritime warfare, Air and aerial defense, Information and command, and Arcane crafts. The final column repeats the subject of the earlier design candidate where one exists. Its presence grants no approval, equipment, starting knowledge or national art entitlement.

## Interpreting gaps and future inventions

Of the 64 country histories, 53 grant exactly the same 13 technologies, including early fighters, carrier fighters, submarines and heavy cruisers. Some have zero convoys, no domestic fleet evidence in the reviewed OOB and no national aviation example. A country with a starting tech ID may have an engine compatibility dependency. That is a separate claim from having pilots, the opportunity to train replacements, or a domestic production design. Keep the technical grant visible in the country ledger while investigating its narrative meaning.

| Design question | What the evidence supports | Technology-design consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can an inland guard state build warships? | A port, dockyard, ship design and operating fleet are distinct. An inherited heavy-cruiser grant supplies only one script ID. Bilgewater has a specific maritime tradition. | Leave unidentified domestic military shipbuilding open. A later coastal concession or licensed design could provide access if the campaign supplies the land, trained crews and workshop. |
| Can every army equip aerial units? | Demacian raptor riders have a rare rider–mount relationship and a reconnaissance role. The generic fighter grant offers no equivalent evidence for northern guards. | Preserve the gap in mass military flight. A trained observer corps could be a new research route where the country has access to a credible aerial platform. |
| Who can exploit a foreign specialty? | Piltover and Zaun have their own original-country facility projects. Their approved Hextech and Shimmer foundations can be researched from 990. Later program stages have distinct gates. | Represent acquiring finished equipment, training operators, maintaining it, and reproducing its design as different outcomes. Check the [signature research decisions](../Twin%20Cities%20Signature%20Research.md) before inventing a transfer route. |
| Do smaller polities share infrastructure? | Named guard OOBs and assigned factory buildings ground an armory and some production. Their workshop output or military supply method often remains unknown. | Start with a cheap armory capability where the OOB supports one. Give nationally specific effects only after checking terrain, available materials, institutions and route consequences. Use the earlier country candidates as questions for that review. |
| Where does the Void belong? | VOI starts with Void creatures and has an authored Resistance/Embrace fork. ICA is a distinct registered core-bearing country. | Keep wardens and specialist musters conditional on their actual route. Do not hand ICA all VOI unlocks or give a generic workshop the power to manufacture living Void creatures. |
| Does a region without a country tag receive country art? | The inspected stack contains no Bandle City regional registration or starting state. Named organizations inside larger regions can still have a campaign role. | A Bandle concept can be considered separately. A country override needs a verified country or holder and a visible technology. Targon’s internal factions do not each acquire a sovereign tag through an art request. |

## Source and resolution notes

1. The [bookmark](../../common/bookmarks/990.txt) starts at 990.1.1.12. The modeled order is installed HOI4, Runeterra, Quarior, then the local Azir checkout. Quarior replaces history/countries and history/states. OOB files in history/units still inherit through all four layers. An identical relative filename resolves to the latest layer. Asset-pack DLC and an opening save are outside this static source model.

2. For 313 top-level states, the country ledger records owner/core effects at the opening date. For the alternative interpretation used by the project auditor, collect all state files including nested directories and retain all candidates at the highest layer for each ID. There are 98 nested Azir files, 383 highest-layer state candidates, and 28 IDs with competing owners. The top-level paths and the candidate paths can be compared in the state ledger. A named core alone makes no promise about playability. Actual control, wartime transfers, on-actions, and resumed saves need separate checks.

3. KAL owns a state in the top-level source, has a local Kalamanda country history and OOB, and carries a Kalmykia registration in the local 00_countries.txt. BLI, BLZ, HAR and KUM also have competing tag-prefixed vanilla and regional country histories. The ledger selects the regional candidate for design and preserves the alternatives explicitly. Do not resolve those conflicts through art or by selecting a convenient file alphabetically.

4. The opening-grant and OOB columns extract direct history commands. Empty means no direct assignment found. An explicitly requested OOB with no matching file is labeled unresolved. GES, GSD, GWS and JGL have no direct OOB assignment in the inspected files. Conditional scripted grants and named focus or event outcomes require a separate trace. Historical and hidden entities may receive additional assignments when activated.

5. References prefixed Game, Runeterra, Quarior or Azir are relative to the corresponding installation. Installation roots are recorded in [Planner.md](../../Planner.md). The conservative owner comparison can be reproduced with the existing [roster auditor](../../tools/runeterra_theme_replacements.py). The candidate and phase-specific examples remain design work. Static evidence does not establish native startup, production, combat, save/reload or multiplayer behavior.
