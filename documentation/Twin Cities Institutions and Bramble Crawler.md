## Bramble Crawler implementation and source assets

The crawler is a line engineering battalion in the armor column. Each is equipped with 12 crawlers. Its initial stats are **3.2 km/h**, **7.2 fuel consumption on the equipment definition**, **+210% jungle movement at battalion level**, a small forest benefit, and penalties in marsh, mountains and at rivers. Division composition and native terrain calculations determine the final speed. The trial template includes four infantry battalions and two crawler battalions. This gives two full crawler allocations from the 24-machine trial batch and a repeatable configuration to test. The clearance effect lives in the division terrain modifiers. It does not write province terrain, open a border, or grant access to Ixtali maps.

The player completes `PIL_design_bramble_crawler` after `PIL_negotiators`, researches `PIL_bramble_crawler_design` under Support Research, and can then manufacture the equipment and edit divisions. Original Piltover researches and first manufactures the crawler. The linked takeover lets Zaun recover the same design after it secures the works. Licensing is disabled. The 45-day trial at River Pilt checks state 53 and military factory capacity. The trial callback `PIL_bramble_crawler.1` offers 30% industry research or 12 army XP with the same one-time batch and model-bound Works Column template.

The initial reference pass reviewed `Runeterra World Art/Zaun x Piltover/piltover-workshops.jpg`, `Loose Files from Downloads/Piltover_Zindelo%27s_Incognium_Runeterra.webp` and `Runeterra World Art/Ixtal/Ixtal-1-1024x562.jpg`. Those earlier references informed the first model. Their visual cues were framed brass, tool-scale ironwork and dense broadleaf growth. The vehicle geometry, material palette and test-strip illustration are original. The model was built in Blender and exported with the installed [IO PDX Mesh](https://github.com/ross-g/io_pdx_mesh) writer. The loader bindings are [model registration](../gfx/entities/PIL_bramble_crawler.gfx), [entity](../gfx/entities/PIL_bramble_crawler.asset), [unit sprites](../interface/PIL_bramble_crawler.gfx) and [trial template](../common/scripted_effects/PIL_bramble_crawler.txt). The map mesh is rigid. Root movement and map scale need native verification.

The local repository ignores `gfx/`. The compiled crawler mesh, material DDS files and three UI textures are present in the working checkout. Keep them together with the entity files when preparing a release asset manifest.

## An alternate outcome to consider

A postwar clearance compact could let Ixtal reopen one damaged route by consent. Ixtal would choose the survey limits, Piltover would decide whether to pay for restoration, and either could withdraw. This would give the crawler a peacetime story without making the existing survey permit a military right of way. It is a proposal for a later 990 branch.

## Zaun's Sumpworks Recovery Company

The recovery company is a 990 municipal design based on the Sump's salvage work, chemtech waste handling and the practical lattice ironwork of the lower city. Riot's *Blitzcrank* biography describes a whole class of hazardous cleanup equipment. Riot's *Orianna* biography shows the people who were first to need respirators and recovery before a failed line. Ekko's story grounds the Firelights' ability to repurpose discarded parts for their wards. The Sump Dredger is a useful cue for the local salvage trade. The joint card region and encounter tables inform the tone of bargains and technical ingenuity. Azir retains its distinct political tags. [Zaun gallery](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/region/zaun/?mv=image-gallery-1) · [Blitzcrank's biography](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_GB/story/champion/blitzcrank/) · [Orianna's biography](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/story/champion/orianna/) · [Ekko's biography](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/story/champion/ekko/) · [Riot's Sump Dredger listing](https://playruneterra.com/en-us/news/game-updates/rotation-2024-full-list/)

Zaun completes **Outfit the Sumpworks Rigs** after **Reopen the Entresol Freight Lift**, then researches **Sumpworks Recovery Engineering**. The research unlocks manufacturing and an armored recovery rig in an engineer support company. A company needs eight rigs. It has **+20% urban movement** and **+30% marsh movement** in its unit definition, with limited terrain attack bonuses and a smaller fuel cost than Piltover's clearance battalion. It shares the engineer slot with Zaun's other engineer company. The unit can be used under any of the three domestic institutions.

**Run the Sumpworks Recovery Trial** tests both wards and a live factory over 35 days. The result grants eight rigs and one model-bound Relief Column template. The player chooses who dispatches the crews first:

- Clinics and ward dispatchers give +2% stability. This is the standard commissioning route.
- The Directorate can prioritize deliveries briefly while contract-backed credit is active. It gets the established delivery-shift bonuses and consumer-goods cost for 60 days.
- The ratified Federation can put Firelight crews on call for 60 days of recovery support while its detour operates.

The icon silhouette is a raised twin-arm derrick over a squat salvage track. The original mesh has a guarded chem-lime pump jacket, riveted fittings and a recovery hook. The trial artwork uses the same model on a raised platform above the Sump water. Its compiled asset paths are [model registration](../gfx/entities/ZAU_sumpworks_recovery.gfx), [entity](../gfx/entities/ZAU_sumpworks_recovery.asset), [sprites](../interface/ZAU_sumpworks_recovery.gfx) and [Relief Column template](../common/scripted_effects/ZAU_sumpworks_recovery.txt).

For owner testing, start fresh as `ZAU`, complete the lift and rig focuses, research and confirm the company occupies the engineer support slot, then run the trial from **Decisions → Zaun's Civic Agenda**. Reload around day 35 and compare the three choice paths in their own eligible saves. On a separate run lose either ward during the timer and verify the canceled trial grants nothing. Train the Relief Column, toggle 3D models, and compare the same host infantry template with and without the recovery company in urban, marsh and jungle. The Sump's province 3334 and the Promenade's province 3406 are mapped as urban. Expect help in the first two terrains and no special jungle modifier. The model and unit effect still need those native checks.
