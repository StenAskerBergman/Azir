# Crystal Scar (CRY) focus tree

## 990 starting premise

Author direction, 26 November 2026. Crystal Scar is a Piltover mining colony. Build its focus tree around the colony and its mining operation. The old Skarner-led Crystal Scar setup is superseded as the basis for the tree.

Author decision, 26 November 2026. Clan Ferros operates the Crystal Scar mine in 990. The appointment applies to the family. The individual managing the mine in 990 remains open.

Riot’s earlier Skarner biography names the Crystal Scar among the brackern valleys and describes people excavating living crystals. Camille’s 2016 biography identifies her great-great-aunt Elicia Ferros as the explorer who found the brackern. Crystal harvesting built the family fortune. These accounts supply the historical basis for choosing Ferros. The colony and the clan’s continued operation of its mine in 990 are the author’s campaign premise. Riot’s current Skarner belongs to Ixtal.
