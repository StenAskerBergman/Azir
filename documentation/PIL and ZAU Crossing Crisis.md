# Piltover and Zaun crossing crisis

The 990 scenario keeps the installed Runeterra country tags. Piltover's game tag is `PLT`, while its new content uses the `PIL` prefix. Zaun uses `ZAU` for both. The two native Balance of Power panels use the same panorama under their original controls and read independent values.

The later institutional reviews, focus icon queue and vehicle source notes are in [Twin Cities institutions, focus art, and the Bramble Crawler](Twin%20Cities%20Institutions%20and%20Bramble%20Crawler.md).

## Local policy

| City | Cooperative end | Neutral | Coercive end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Piltover | A Charter for Both Cities | Permits and Precedent | The Council's Prerogative |
| Zaun | The Ward Compact | An Uneasy Ward Bargain | The Glasc Directorate |

Both bars start at zero. The political focus branches can move the local bar by 15 points before a city commits at the crossing. An open freight charter sets that city's bar to −65, reserves working capacity for the other city, and costs local factory output while the joint schedule is pending. Exclusive control sets that city's bar to +65 and leaves its freight register closed. A city may take a different crossing position from its domestic faction. Each player can see the other city's public commitment through the prerequisites for the available crossing decisions.

## Focus and decision path

1. Piltover completes **The City of Progress**, **Commission the Canal Survey**, and **Appoint the Crossing Delegation**.
2. Zaun completes **Meet at the Last Drop**, **Inspect the Air Shafts**, **Reopen the Entresol Freight Lift**, and **Seat the Ward Engineers**.
3. Piltover selects **Commission the Canal Survey** under **Decisions → The Twin Cities** and sends the inspection order. Zaun answers the accompanying event. Answering permits both governments to declare their policies. Sending the ward engineers additionally costs political power and adds the engineers to the inspection.
4. Each country opens **Politics → Balance of Power** and chooses its policy. Piltover can publish the survey and open the freight register, or put the crossing under Council permit. Zaun can share the hoist register and reserve freight space, or reserve the yards for Zaun. Each declaration costs 25 political power.

## Four multiplayer outcomes

Use a save from immediately after Zaun answers the inspection and before either side declares policy. Both countries need their local focus prerequisites. Reload that checkpoint for each outcome.

| Piltover | Zaun | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cooperative | Cooperative | Both receive **The Two Cities Accord**. Complete **Fund the Upper Approach** and **Fund the Lower Approach**. Either player can publish **Reopen the Crossing** once. Both receive lasting factory output, research and consumer-goods benefits while the original approach states remain under their signatories in peacetime. |
| Coercive | Cooperative | Piltover can **Impose Exclusive Freight Permits** after Zaun opens its register and reserves freight space. Zaun receives **They Look Down on Us**. Zaun's response closes its register, cancels Piltover's temporary advantage and shifts Zaun's own bar toward coercion. |
| Cooperative | Coercive | Zaun can **Take Control of the Shared Loading Slots** after Piltover opens its register and reserves freight space. Piltover receives **Permits Seized at the Crossing**. Piltover can withdraw access and divert freight, ending Zaun's advantage and shifting Piltover's own bar. |
| Coercive | Coercive | Completing the second declaration gives both a freight disruption. Either can fortify its own approach and pursue its detour focus and decision. There is no scripted war. |

For either betrayal, first check the attacker before the other player opens its register. The exploit must remain unavailable. After the concession, it costs 30 political power and grants up to 120 days of priority freight. The victim can pay 20 political power to suspend access and begin temporary emergency rerouting before building a permanent detour. A detour costs 65 political power and adds a continuing consumer-goods burden. Fortifying in mutual hardening costs 45 political power. Recheck that only the aggressor receives priority freight, only the victim's bar moves in the response, and a closed concession cannot be exploited again.

If one country is AI-controlled, its domestic institutions, opening preference, and the other country's declared policy all affect its decision weights. The AI prioritizes a response to betrayal. Check at least one run with each country under AI control and check save/reload after the first commitment and after a settled outcome.
