# Icathian Resistance and the two creeds

The Resistance wins the same war for two different reasons. The Hybrid wants someone left alive to rebuild. The Purist wants the people who rebuild to inherit a world free of the thing that destroyed it. Both remember Icathia. They disagree about what it means to let it fall a second time.

This is a design and dialogue reference for the 990 campaign. Resistance and Embrace remain the opening choices. Hybrid and Purist are proposed doctrines within Resistance. The harsher views below belong to the characters and factions speaking them.

## What happened at Icathia

- **Shurima conquered Icathia before the rebellion.** The old Mage King Axamuk opposed Shuriman expansion. After the conquest, Icathia had a governing council under Shuriman rule. Centuries later, Zilean sought allies in Kahleek, Kalduga, and Ixtal. They refused to defy Shurima. The council backed the newly discovered power and crowned a new Mage King. Keep the old king separate from **Axamuk Var-Choi Kohari Icath’or**, the narrator and newly sworn Kohari in Graham McNeill's battle story. The sources here do not establish the name of the later crowned king. [Zilean's biography, Riot text mirror](https://therune.lol/lore/champions/zilean/) · [Where Icathia Once Stood, Riot text mirror](https://luis.toino.pt/story/where-icathia-once-stood)
- **The mages discovered a breach and chose to unleash what they found.** The earthquake at Saabera exposed the power. Saijax escorted the mages and saw how poorly they could contain it. At Icathia, the mages and priests unleashed the Void against the invading Ascended Host and lost control. The council's wager and the battlefield act are both part of the story. Kai’Sa and Kassadin belong to a much later generation. A Purist can accuse them of repeating the old council's logic. The ancient responsibility belongs to the people who made the decision. [Jax's biography, Riot text mirror](https://therune.lol/lore/champions/jax/) · [Where Icathia Once Stood](https://luis.toino.pt/story/where-icathia-once-stood)
- **Zilean opposed the decision. Saijax helped bring the discovery home and came to regret it.** Zilean's biography says he would have preferred the rebellion crushed to seeing the Void unleashed. The council proceeded. The account does not establish a formal vote or give us the wording of a protest. **Saijax Cail-Rynx Kohari Icath’un** saw what might happen, escorted the mages anyway, joined the reconstituted Kohari, and fought Shurima. The biography gives him explicit regret over letting the mages live on the road from Saabera. Whether he could have stopped the plan remains unknown. His discarded brazier-stave carried elemental flame that hurt the Void. Zilean saved people in his tower by taking it out of time and still has to find a way to bring them back. Their methods and failures should stay distinct. [Jax's biography](https://therune.lol/lore/champions/jax/) · [Zilean's biography](https://therune.lol/lore/champions/zilean/)
- **The war continued after the city fell.** Saijax and his fellow Kohari Colgrim Avel-Essa describe bringing down a god-warrior at Bai-Zhek. Setaka led the Shuriman assault and was caught when the rift opened. Aatrox answered her call and later fought alongside the Ascended who burned shut the largest rifts. Kassadin credits **Horok**, bearer of the Nether Blade, with the first telling blow against the enemy's masters and showing the Ascended how to fight the abyss. The Ixtali Ascended **Ne’Zuk** built the living-stone Monolith to strike at the source. It crashed and exposed more of the Void. **Malphite** survived as its last shard. The survivors had several ways of fighting and suffered real failures. [Where Icathia Once Stood](https://luis.toino.pt/story/where-icathia-once-stood) · [Aatrox's biography](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/story/champion/aatrox/) · [Whom Does the Desert Know?](https://therune.lol/lore/stories/kassadin-color-story/) · [Malphite's biography, Riot text mirror](https://therune.lol/lore/champions/malphite/)
- **The Darkin warning describes a longer descent.** The war scarred the Ascended. The seer twins Shabaka and Shabake were badly affected by what they encountered while sealing the Great Rift. Shurima's collapse after Azir, feuding, and forbidden sorcery preceded the later sealing of Darkin in weapons. Aatrox's fate is a warning a Purist can invoke. A narrator should give the intervening history room to breathe. A more accurate version of the supplied line is “They came back from the Void. It took another war to seal them in steel.” [The Legend of the Darkin](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/story/the-legend-of-the-darkin/) · [Twilight of the Gods, Riot text mirror](https://luis.toino.pt/story/twilight-of-the-gods)

### Rift, Gate, Voidborn and Watchers

The ancient battlefield breach and Azir's 990 Gate serve different dates and purposes. The ancient precedent is the decision to wield the Void at all. The modern Gate at the Rupture gives the Embrace route a physical way to repeat that decision. *Rift Rapture* begins construction and reveals the first modern breach. *Open Void Gate* escalates it. The event can call it the first rift of the current Gate without implying no rift ever existed before. [Gate effects](../common/scripted_effects/void_gate.txt) · [Gate events](../events/void_gate.txt)

At the Howling Abyss, Lissandra's containment of the Watchers gives the Purists a second precedent and a harder question. Lissandra had already sought power from the Watchers before sealing them at the cost of her sisters and their armies. The Watchers stir again. Containment can be costly and temporary even when the defender refuses to open another breach. **Vel’Koz is Voidborn**, gathering knowledge that serves the Watchers. **Bel’Veth is a rival Voidborn power** who wants to defeat her progenitors and remake the world herself. In *Pinwheel*, her offer to Kai’Sa buys humanity time on Bel’Veth's terms. This is the sharpest test of “Any Edge Matters.” Kai’Sa using the skin she survived with, Kassadin carrying a weapon he chose, and a government bargaining with Bel’Veth each represent a different commitment. A future scene can give the Purists grounds to fear where the next concession leads while keeping those acts distinct. Closing the Icathian Gate and containing the Freljord's Watchers also have distinct outcomes. ## The creeds in 990

### Hybrid — Any Edge Matters

Kai’Sa and Kassadin fight to keep people alive through the next breach. They accept the possibility of losing more of themselves in the process. The Hybrids think the Purists can afford to wait only because someone else is already down there. **Victory at any cost** lands hardest when the two characters can name the cost they have already paid and still say yes.

- **Kai’Sa** survived by bonding with a living Void carapace. She hunts the things that killed her family and protects people who may recoil from her. In *Monstrous*, the armor hungers and she recognizes a disturbing impulse in herself. In *Hollowspun*, Taliyah saves her and civilians make room for her. Both experiences matter. Her danger is real, and so is the person capable of choosing whom she protects. Give her civilians to get out and a line she can draw on what the Resistance may ask of them. [Kai’Sa's biography](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_US/story/champion/kaisa/) · [Monstrous, Riot text mirror](https://luis.toino.pt/story/kaisa-monstrous-story) · [Hollowspun, Riot text mirror](https://luis.toino.pt/story/hollowspun)
- **Kassadin** sought forbidden equipment after his family disappeared. His Zaunite engineering, Ionian blessings, foreign protective stone, and Horok's weapon came by different means. He is willing to carry them into the abyss with little regard for what the expedition does to him. His starting motive is vengeance against Malzahar. A reunion in 990 would give him someone whose survival matters more than his own death. The mod can use their established father–daughter relationship while leaving Kai’Sa the choice to join or keep her own watch. Riot's biographies and *Pinwheel* support the implied kinship. The mod authors the reunion and its circumstances. [Kassadin's biography](https://universe.leagueoflegends.com/en_GB/story/champion/kassadin/) · [Whom Does the Desert Know?](https://therune.lol/lore/stories/kassadin-color-story/) · [Pinwheel](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Pinwheel) · [Kai’Sa return event](../events/azir_kaisa.txt)

### Purist — Defend what we cannot afford to lose

The Purists see modern expediency through the memory of the old council. To them the promise of one final shortcut is the danger. At the harsh end of this doctrine they see the Hybrids as casualties still walking, feel pity for them, and believe destroying every remaining trace is their duty. “No exceptions” should come from someone looking at an actual person who saved lives. It should cost something for the player to believe it.

- **Jax** remembers escorting a weapon he distrusted and fighting on the day it opened. He can speak with specific shame. His answer is to hold ground, train fighters, and carry the elemental flame that already proved useful. The existing commander trait Ambusher supports holding approaches. His severe turn in the mod could begin with the thought that he already stood aside once. Riot's Jax recruits and tests potential allies. A decision to kill a resisting survivor like Kai’Sa needs a visible 990 turning point if Jax is to own it. [Jax's biography](https://therune.lol/lore/champions/jax/) · [None Shall Pass, Riot text mirror](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/None_Shall_Pass) · [The Man Without a Weapon](../localisation/english/_icathia_l_english.yml#L117)
- **Zilean** remembers a council promising victory and a tower full of people he still cannot bring home. His doctrine is strongest when he can distinguish an apparent win from a future his people can inhabit. He already knows a rescue can carry a terrible cost. His opposition to deploying the Void is established. A doctrine of killing every exposed person would be a 990 political choice with consequences, especially for the mage who is still trying to rescue people others might have written off. ## A decision the player can feel

**Start with a concrete rescue at the Rupture.** The Resistance has the Wardens and a route to Kai’Sa already. Give those people a shared emergency after the opening split. Put something under the threatened ground that matters to both camps. The Hybrid can bring people out now by carrying an exposure or a debt that will remain after the rescue. The Purist can seal the route with disciplined fighters and independent magic, with a real cost in people, time, or ground. Both plans can work. Let the player see what each saved and what each left behind.

**Make the wider threats ask different questions.** Against a brood, Kai’Sa's knowledge can get people through terrain the Wardens cannot yet hold. Against the Watchers, Bel’Veth can offer time with a price the player can understand. The Purists can mount a longer defense or support containment on independent terms. The same equipment, flag and reward should not make these decisions interchangeable. Let the campaign show who paid for the advantage, and whether the people at risk had any say.

**Keep two open design decisions visible.**

1. Does the official Purist doctrine culminate in killing exposed allies by default, or is that the hardline outcome after a breach or betrayal? In either version, Jax and Zilean should face a decision with a named person and a consequence. Their moral premise can be shared while their personal limits differ.
2. Can the Hybrid spend other people's lives and consent for survival, or does “any cost” start with what Kai’Sa and Kassadin personally volunteer and then escalate? Kai’Sa protecting civilians and Kassadin risking himself give the doctrine its emotional center. Bel’Veth's bargain tests whether it stays there.
