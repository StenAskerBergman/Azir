# Shuriman generic pass — active playset audit

25 November 2026. These findings cover Azir over Runeterra – Quarior patch and Runeterra, with the installed HOI4 assets available underneath. Starting appointments come from effective country history. Generic military portraits come from the country pool or its capital continent. A flag or champion portrait on disk does not establish that a generated recruit uses it. Proposed next steps below require a casting or production decision unless already approved in the [pass plan](990%20Character%20Portrait%20and%20GFX%20To-Do.md).
