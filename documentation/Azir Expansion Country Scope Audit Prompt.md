# Azir Expansion country scope audit

Copy this prompt into the chat coordinating the audit. Keep the same scope and source snapshot when handing out worker assignments. The default is the 990 scenario and the Shuriman region, including neighboring countries where their existing content directly affects a Shuriman route. Set the scope to **all implemented Runeterra countries** for a whole-map audit.

## Prompt

I want a source-backed map of what is actually left to do in my Runeterra HOI4 mod. The Azir Expansion should bring Shurima to life in a way that feels believable within Runeterra Prime. I want the story each country tells about itself to hold up when I look at the map, take a focus, choose a side, or see what the AI does. Use GPT‑6.2 Sol with the lowest available reasoning setting for the bulk of the work. Bring unresolved design decisions back to me.

### Authority and scope

- This is a read-only audit. Preserve my authored routes, choices, mechanics, writing, art, and unfinished work. Leave anything I marked complete closed. If a neighboring route depends on a closed element, report the dependency with evidence and leave the decision to me. A packet, backlog card, existing script, or previous agent suggestion does not grant approval to adopt, rewrite, repair, or extend it.
- Work in the existing local checkout. Read `AGENTS.md` and `Planner.md` first. Read only the relevant sections of the current sprint documents, country packets, and Trello cards or receipts. Treat those as evidence of design intent and prior verification. Attribute a decision to me only when an author instruction or acceptance record supports it. If a source is unavailable, record what remains uncertain and continue.
- Identify which build you are assessing. Use the 990 bookmark at `990.1.1.12` by default. Start from the live Azir checkout, inspect its descriptor and dependency order, and use the relevant Runeterra and Quarior files where they supply active content. The integrated source under `E:/Local Repo/Runeterra/Runeterra` and any frozen candidate are distinct sources. Record their paths and revisions if used, and identify local changes that affect the evidence. Where the effective definition is uncertain, show the competing definitions and mark precedence unresolved. Leave branches, working files, Trello, saves, and the game untouched. Return reports in this chat. Contact another Codex chat only with my explicit authorization in this chat for that recipient and purpose.
- Build the country roster before assigning deep reviews. Cross-check country tags, the bookmark, country and state history, tree selection, and actual release or civil-war entry points. Give each scoped tag one row with effective layer, start/activation status, assigned content, and scope reason. Distinguish 990 playable countries, existing off-bookmark countries, releaseable or civil-war tags, shared/generic recipients, and definitions with no demonstrated entry. Record the last category separately from playable work. Keep neighbor-only coverage bounded to the Shurima-facing connection. Treat an unregistered lore polity as context unless I ask to add it.

### Share the work without duplicating it

Have one coordinator build the roster, distribute a common evidence baseline, and consolidate findings. Use GPT‑6.2 Sol at the lowest available reasoning setting for each worker if the model can be selected. Report any fallback. Use in-chat subagents where supported and permitted, or process the same work packets sequentially. Give each worker one country or one tightly coupled group of small tags with a named boundary. Give shared trees and cross-tag chains one reporting owner, then record the affected tags on the same finding. Send the worker the audited revision/layers, tag, entry points, and any author-locked or deferred items. Reuse source notes across workers.

Workers should start with the active entry points and sample each distinct route or starting role. Follow references that could change the finding. Expand the sample where it reveals a broken link, an unclear endpoint, or a meaningful narrative conflict. Research lore when it establishes a country’s premise or changes a decision. Use short findings and direct references. Mark unsampled branches, unresolved references, and uncertain lore instead of spending a second pass rechecking unchanged evidence. Where a cheap worker cannot settle a consequential question, return the exact question and sources to the coordinator.

### Work packet for each country

1. **Establish what the player gets.** Trace initial leadership and other recruited roles, state control and cores, OOB and starting spirits, then the reachable focuses, decisions, events, laws, diplomatic responses, and route endpoints relevant to the country. Follow actual prerequisites, callers, scope, effects, AI weights, and localization. Check relevant art bindings and files when they carry story information or block delivery. A defined focus, event, or portrait is evidence of presence. A traced entry and outcome establish static reachability for the path inspected. Report native startup, visuals, progression, save/reload, and multiplayer separately when evidence exists for the assessed build. For a material gap, give the exact player-run test from the 990 start or appropriate activation path, with expected result. Follow `AGENTS.md` for previewing any proposed test launch.
2. **Write the country's narrative in a few sentences.** Describe its starting circumstances, institutions and people with agency, the pressure that forces a choice, and what each existing major route asks the player to do and live with. Carry that same thread from the 990 setup through the choices and their consequences. Fit the form to the country. A small settlement, releasable tag, or shared-tree recipient may need only a credible local identity, start state, and regional role. Preserve what makes each country distinct.
3. **Anchor claims in the right evidence.** For the fictional world, start with relevant Riot-published stories, biographies, regional pages, comics, and cinematics. Use League lore wikis to locate works and cross-check names, geography, and chronology. Cite the original work where available and identify when a wiki is the only accessible source. Label each consequential claim **Runeterra Prime established**, **author-set 990 premise**, **plausible inference**, **conflicting or uncertain**, or **unsupported in sources checked**. Riot lore establishes the world and character constraints. My 990 setup establishes this campaign's starting conditions. A believable alternate outcome can follow from player choices if the route supplies the people, means, motivation, and consequences to support it. Check the continuity of card flavor, promotional scenes, and adaptations before relying on them. When published accounts conflict, cite both and leave the conflict open. Prefer the narrowest supported default, especially for dates, allegiances, institutions, and borders. Leave sparse lore sparse.
4. **Check what the game teaches through play.** For passive storytelling, check what the map, ownership versus claims, names, portraits, starting armies, national spirits, buildings, and bookmark descriptions imply before the player acts. For active storytelling, check what focus choices, events, decisions, diplomacy, costs, rewards, and AI behavior actually allow or incentivize. *Harmony* means those signals are consistent with each other and the authored premise. *Resonance* means the player experiences the country's stakes through the decisions, tradeoffs, and consequences. For a dissonance finding, identify the exact signal or promise, what the game actually does, and the conflicting source or author premise. For example, if the text celebrates a voluntary pact, trace whether the counterpart can actually refuse and what the sender receives on acceptance versus refusal. Preserve authored tensions, unreliable claims, and intentional alternate outcomes where the game gives the player enough context to understand them.
5. **Identify the smallest remaining question or task.** Give each finding a primary disposition: **confirmed gap**, **verification needed**, **author decision**, **deferred**, or **closed**. Flag important areas you could not inspect as **unassessed** in the coverage notes. For a confirmed gap, cite the existing intended behavior and the broken or absent link. For an author decision, explain the choice in plain language and what existing work each option affects. If the author-set premise and the published lore diverge, document where they diverge and whether the game supplies a credible bridge. Where appropriate, identify the smallest possible future repair or the option to keep the current behavior. Do not author new branches, characters, settlements, mechanics, dialogue, or localization during this audit.

### Deliver one consolidated scope map

1. **Start with the decisions I need to make.** List the handful that unlock the most work. Give the tags, the exact choice, the best supporting evidence, and what stays pending until I decide. Report zero if there are none.
2. **Give me the country roster.** One row per scoped tag, with its role in 990, current entry/route coverage, meaningful remaining work, highest verification reached, and dependencies. Explicitly list any tags or routes still unassessed and why.
3. **Give me a short country brief for each audited tag or group.** Include its narrative throughline, the key Runeterra Prime and 990 anchors, the most consequential examples of passive and active alignment or dissonance, and relevant closed or deferred boundaries. Provide `file:line` or stable script IDs for gameplay claims, direct links and work titles for lore claims, and the exact author note or card for intent.
4. **Give me a deduplicated backlog in dependency order.** For each item include **tag/route | disposition | impact | evidence | prerequisite | smallest next step | proof of done**. Put launch, route and shared-chain blockers first, followed by material story or presentation gaps. Keep native verification and author decisions visible even when the work already exists in source. Defer optional flavor that adds no necessary country identity or progression. Explicitly name what remains unverified and which build the report covers.

Start by returning the roster and proposed batches, then continue through the audit in the same task. Bring choices that require my judgment into the final report and complete the independent inspection while those choices remain open. Stop at the scope map.
